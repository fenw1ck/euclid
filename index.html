Muscle Trainer

Drag the picture to move it; zoom in with ＋ or by holding the left mouse button. Peel the muscles away layer by layer to reach the bones. Tap a muscle to see where it attaches, or tap a bone to see which muscles attach to it. Pick a level to choose which muscles to study: Core, Standard, Advanced or Expert. Each level includes the ones before it. 24 bones, drawn as a simplified schematic.
